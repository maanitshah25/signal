# Signal

Track any researcher with one click and get a feed of their new papers, citation spikes, grants, and patents, right in Chrome's side panel.

Signal runs on your own [TinyFish](https://www.tinyfish.ai) API key. Nothing passes through servers we operate.

- [Privacy policy](privacy/)
- [Source code on GitHub](https://github.com/maanitshah25/signal)
- [Report an issue](https://github.com/maanitshah25/signal/issues)

## How it works

1. Open a researcher's Google Scholar or university profile.
2. Signal detects the researcher in the side panel. Google Scholar profiles are read locally for free.
3. Click **+ Track**. TinyFish agents gather recent papers, citation activity, grants, patents, and collaborations.
4. Signal re-checks tracked researchers once a day and notifies you when a new paper appears.
